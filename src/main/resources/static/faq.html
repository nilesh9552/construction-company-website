FAQ

Common questions, simple answers

Find quick answers about cost, time, materials, and the project process.

How much will it cost?

The cost depends on plot size, floors, materials, and finish level. We give a custom estimate after a site visit.

How long will it take?

Time depends on the work size. Small interiors may take 3–6 weeks, while a full home may take longer.

Who buys the materials?

We can help arrange materials from trusted vendors, and you can choose the quality level you want.
